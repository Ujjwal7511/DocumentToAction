# Decisions Must Record Owner and Due Date

**Standard ID:** PM-STD-003  
**Applies to:** Meeting notes, ADRs, project updates

## Rule

Every project decision that creates follow-up work must record:

- A named **owner**
- A **due date** or target milestone
- The decision outcome in one clear sentence

## Rule for action items

Proposed action items without an owner or deadline must not be treated as
committed work until a human reviewer assigns both and explicitly approves them.

## Rationale

Ownerless decisions accumulate as silent debt. This standard keeps the audit
trail actionable without allowing automated task assignment.
